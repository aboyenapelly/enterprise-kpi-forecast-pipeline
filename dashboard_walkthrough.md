# Dashboard Walkthrough

## Page 1: Executive KPI Overview
Recommended visuals:
- Revenue trend by month
- Gross margin rate by category
- Units sold by product category
- Exception count by data quality check

## Page 2: Supply Chain and Inventory View
Recommended visuals:
- Inventory movement by product category
- Stockout risk by warehouse
- Slow-moving product view
- Supplier late delivery trend

## Page 3: Forecast-Ready Dataset View
Recommended visuals:
- Monthly demand by product
- Rolling three-month demand
- Lagged demand feature availability
- Forecast-ready record count

## Business Narrative
The dashboard is designed to help business teams understand where performance is changing, where reporting data needs correction, and which datasets are ready for planning or forecasting workflows.
